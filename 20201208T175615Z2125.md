**My Surface Pro 7's screen starts ghosting** (displaying persistent image), displays interlaced brightness pattern, and the top few lines are darker than usual. This note bookmarks the links related to this problem.

- [🔖 Microsoft Community (2018). “Surface Pro 4 flickering bad & horizontal lines, how to fix?”](https://answers.microsoft.com/en-us/surface/forum/surfpro4-surfdrivers/surface-pro-4-flickering-bad-horizontal-lines-how/5524bff9-221d-4f6d-a683-d777db70b73e)

Apparently this is called **Flickergate** and [even has its own website](https://flickergate.com/).

- [🔖 Ste Wright (2018). “Brightness flickering on your Surface Laptop or Surface Pro? Here's a fix”](https://www.stewright.me/2018/01/fix-for-flickering-brightness-on-surface-device-display/) suggests disabling Intel DPST.
  - [🔖 Disable Intel DPST - UPDATED to work for all devices | r/Surface](https://www.reddit.com/r/Surface/comments/adczt5/disable_intel_dpst_updated_to_work_for_all_devices/)
  - [🔖 how to disable “intel display power saving technology” | Super User](https://superuser.com/questions/1487069/how-to-disable-intel-display-power-saving-technology)
  - My previous value was 512. Changed to 528.
  - Brightness still flickers.
- [🔖 Fix for Surface Pro 7 screen brightness flickering? | r/Surface](https://www.reddit.com/r/Surface/comments/dn649a/fix_for_surface_pro_7_screen_brightness_flickering/) suggests getting Intel Graphics Command Center app, and disabling Display Power Saving.
  - I did not see that option, but disabling "Panel Self Refresh" seems to stop it.
- [🔖 Intel Iris Plus Graphics Driver causes flickering on Microsoft Surface Pro 7 | Intel Community](https://community.intel.com/t5/Graphics/Intel-Iris-Plus-Graphics-Driver-causes-flickering-on-Microsoft/td-p/1209001) suggests installing a "generic driver".
  - My Surface is installed with "Intel(R) Iris(R) Plus Graphics" version "26.20.100.7641"
  - Downloading latest version "27.20.100.8935" as of writing (2020-12-09)
