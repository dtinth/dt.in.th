I just migrated [notes.dt.in.th](AboutNotes) from Nuxt.js to Next.js. Main reason being that [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) plays well with [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) better than Nuxt.

**Pull request:** <https://github.com/dtinth/notes.dt.in.th/pull/2>

However, there are some challenges. My notes are written in [VuePress-style markdown](https://vuepress.vuejs.org/guide/markdown.html). That means it is possible to [use Vue in Markdown](https://vuepress.vuejs.org/guide/using-vue.html#templating), as I have used in [these notes](20220724T171557Z9306).
