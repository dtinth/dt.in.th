---
title: Client-side-only components in Next.js
public: true
---

To render a [React](React) component only on client-side in [Next.js](Next), use [next/dynamic](https://nextjs.org/docs/advanced-features/dynamic-import#with-no-ssr) with `{ ssr: false }`.

## Further reading

- [Piumi Liyana Gunawardhana (2022). “Using Non-SSR Friendly Components with Next.js.” _Bits and Pieces_](https://blog.bitsrc.io/using-non-ssr-friendly-components-with-next-js-916f38e8992c)
- [“Dynamic Import.” _Next.js docs_](https://nextjs.org/docs/advanced-features/dynamic-import)
