:::lead
This year (2026), I'm following [Tyndale's _One-Year Bible_](https://sites.tyndale.com/oneyearbible/) [Reading Plan](https://bibleinayearonline.com/readingplan/bibleinayearreadingplan.pdf), while listening to [_The Word of Promise_ Audio Bible app](https://www.wordofpromiseapp.com/) [^wop].
:::

The plan is widely available in many Bible apps, including the beautifully-designed [Life Bible](https://lifebible.com/) App which I am using this year. However, _The Word of Promise_ app doesn't come with it.

Thankfully, the app does have a custom reading plan feature where you can create and share plans via PL2 files [^format]. I created a PL2 file based on the publicly available reading schedule so you can import it into The Word of Promise Audio Bible App and follow along.

[^wop]: [_The Word of Promise_](https://www.thomasnelson.com/9781418576738/the-word-of-promise-audio-bible-complete-bible-hear-the-bible-come-alive-nkjv/) is a high-production audio Bible featuring the New King James Version (NKJV) rendered as dramatic audio theater, featuring an original score, world-class sound effects, and performances by famous actors.
[^format]: The PL2 file format is XML-based, so it's straightforward to write a simple script to generate it based on the publicly available reading plan.
