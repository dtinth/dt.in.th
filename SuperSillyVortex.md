# super-silly-vortex

A silly recreation of the **Rain Vortex** at Jewel Changi Airport, but it's `node_modules` instead of water.

Made at [Super Silly Hackathon 2019](https://supersillyhackathon.sg).

::youtube[8as2nAU6cZA]

::cta[Presentation slides]{href="https://github.com/dtinth/super-silly-vortex/tree/master/presentation"}
::cta[Open the demo]{href="https://super-silly-vortex.netlify.com/"}

## Motivation

I had a spark of motivation after seeing this tweet:

:::tweet

<p lang="en" dir="ltr">Good news everyone, I finally found where all of our node_modules go! They're in the Jewel Rain Vortex at the Singapore airport. <a href="https://t.co/53j8MnMThI">pic.twitter.com/53j8MnMThI</a></p><img src="https://im.dt.in.th/ipfs/bafybeiawgeciq4mb6jvwtjc3mwtxcpxnzydobpr4pj565qeelchhna47fu/image.webp">&mdash; Monica Dinculescu (@notwaldorf) <a href="https://twitter.com/notwaldorf/status/1140336804026937344?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw">June 16, 2019</a>

:::

I wanted to create a visualization of what `yarn install` does to your filesystem but make it looks like files are falling down from Changi Jewel's Rain Vortex (instead of water).

The end result is a 3D recreation of the Jewel using THREE.js where filenames dropping down from the top whenever a file is created on the file system.
