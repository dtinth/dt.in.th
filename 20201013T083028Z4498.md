**Apparently perfumed alcohol sprays are a thing…** After trying out several alcohol-based hand sanitizers, I have found that some have a very pleasant smell, not too different from a perfume. Some are so-and-so. Others are just… unbearable. In this note I list my findings.

:::::

## Pleasant-smelling ones

:::::split

### Hug Organic Sanitizer Spray

Spotted at ECOTOPIA in Siam Discovery, [Hug Organic Sanitizer Spray](https://hugorganic.com/product-category/hand-sanitizer-spray/) has the fragrance of **tea tree**, **eucalyptus**, **rose water** and **lavender**. Available in many sizes. 70% v/v.

- THB 120 / 60 ㎤ (0.5 ㎤/฿)
- THB 495 / 500 ㎤ (1.01 ㎤/฿)
- THB 850 / 1000 ㎤ (1.18 ㎤/฿)

This is my current favorite. The scent is just about right and not too strong.

:::::

:::::split

### Beauty Cryst Sanitizing Alc Spray

This spray is a complementary gift when staying at Mandarin Hotel. Contains **aloe vera** and “fragrance.” Smells pretty good, albeit a bit strong. 75% v/v.

:::::

:::::split

### Bellez’a Paris Alcohol Spray

Spotted at the Perfect Stranger cafe. It comes with 3 variants: **New York**, **Paris** and **London**. After trying out the demos, I like the Paris one the most and bought it. 76.4% v/v.

THB 85 / 30 ㎤ (0.35 ㎤/฿)

### After You Touch Me Shibuya Honey Toast 75% Alcohol Hand Moisturizing Sanitizer

Who would have thought it, an alcohol spray that smells like **honey toast**. Spotted at After You. Some people might not like the smell, but I think it is fine (in a weird way). 75% v/v.

THB 65 / 20 ㎤ (0.30 ㎤/฿)

::::aside
![a credit-card-shaped hand sanitizer spray](https://static.dt.in.th/uploads/2020/10/13/hand-sanitizer-aft.jpg)

### After You Touch Me Strawberry Kakigoori 75% Alcohol Hand Moisturizing Sanitizer

Another one by After You. It has a nice **strawberry** smell, although some compare it to a _scented eraser_. 75% v/v.

THB 65 / 20 ㎤ (0.30 ㎤/฿)

:::::

:::::split

### Kalmme Hand Sanitizing Spray

This one has **aloe vera** extract, **eucaalyptus** and **tea tree** essential oil. The scent is quite faint.

:::::

## Not a spray but still pleasant-smelling

:::::split

### Siribuncha Original Ethyl Alcohol 70% v/v

This one is not a spray, but you can refill your spray container with this.
It gives _that_ kind of smell that I have associated with cleanliness… you know, the unique kind of smell you only find in hospitals and stuff. Yes, that kind of thing.

Available in most drug stores.

THB 45 / 450 ㎤ (10 ㎤/฿)
