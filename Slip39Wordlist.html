The SLIP39 wordlist, originally designed for sharing ‘master secrets’ in a human-friendly way, consists of 1024 common words. Each word is 4–8 letters long, with unique 4-letter prefixes to prevent ambiguity, designed to be easily distinguished from each other, both in spelling and pronunciation. Each randomly selected word contributes 10 bits of entropy to the generated phrase.
