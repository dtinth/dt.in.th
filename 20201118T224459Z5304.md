**Ed25519** [is an EdDSA signature scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EdDSA) for digital signatures.

Uses:

- [As an SSH key](https://medium.com/risan/upgrade-your-ssh-key-to-ed25519-c6e8d60d3c54) ([with vanity addresses](VanityEd25519SSHKey))
- [As an algorithm](20201015T185602Z2963) for [JWT](JWT)
- As a general-purpose message signature system ([TweetNaCl.js](https://tweetnacl.js.org/#/sign))
