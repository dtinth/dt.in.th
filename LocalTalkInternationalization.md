<div class="lead">

**In Thailand, many local meetups talks are spoken in Thai language.** That doesn’t mean it can’t reach an international audience.

</div>

When I present at meetups, I speak Thai, as it’s easier to get the message across to my primary audience in Thai. However, I also do these to “internationalize” my talks when publishing the video to YouTube:

- Talk is in Thai, but slides are in English.
- Add English subtitles to the video. [The first draft can be automated.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MuTlQJOVcH4)
- [Add localized titles and descriptions to the video.](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6289575)

## Inspirations

- [Masahiro Sakurai on Creating Games](https://www.youtube.com/@sora_sakurai_en) — The spoken language is Japanese, but all the text is in English. He also has a [second channel](https://www.youtube.com/@sora_sakurai_jp) with the same content in Japanese.
- [CodeWithHarry](https://www.youtube.com/@CodeWithHarry) — Even though he has a [second channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ProgrammingWithHarry) which is English only, yet in his main channel where he speaks in Hindi, all on-screen text is in English.
