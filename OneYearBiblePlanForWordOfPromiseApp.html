This year (2026), I'm following Tyndale's One-Year Bible Reading Plan, while listening to The Word of Promise Audio Bible app 1.

The plan is widely available in many Bible apps, including the beautifully-designed Life Bible App which I am using this year. However, The Word of Promise app doesn't come with it.

Thankfully, the app does have a custom reading plan feature where you can create and share plans via PL2 files 2. I created a PL2 file based on the publicly available reading schedule so you can import it into The Word of Promise Audio Bible App and follow along.

Download Reading Plan Filehttps://drive.google.com/file/d/1iC25x22H_knyZDZR6TK01P7eJU5CKVdO/view?usp=sharing

Happy New Year, and I hope this is useful for your Bible reading journey in 2026.

Footnotes

  1. The Word of Promise is a high-production audio Bible featuring the New King James Version (NKJV) rendered as dramatic audio theater, featuring an original score, world-class sound effects, and performances by famous actors. ↩

  2. The PL2 file format is XML-based, so it's straightforward to write a simple script to generate it based on the publicly available reading plan. ↩
