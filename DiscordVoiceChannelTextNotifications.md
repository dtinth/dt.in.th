title: Discord text chat in voice channels notifications behavior
---

In [Discord](Discord), when you send a [text message in a voice channel](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/4412085582359-Text-Channels-Text-Chat-In-Voice-Channels#h_01FMJT3SP072ZFJCZWR0EW6CJ1), people will get a notification if:

- they are connected to the voice channel (including deafened), or
- they are directly mentioned in the message, even if they are not connected to the voice channel.
