[**Bun**](https://bun.sh/) is a [JavaScript](JavaScript) and [TypeScript](TypeScript) toolkit that aims to provide an all-in-one solution for building server-side apps. It includes a runtime designed as a drop-in replacement for [Node.js](Node), among other features.
